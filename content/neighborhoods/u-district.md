The Ave (University Way) is a long run of inexpensive restaurants that punches well above the neighborhood's reputation. The University of Washington campus is worth walking regardless of student status — the Quad's cherry blossoms in late March and early April are a genuine city event, and the view down Rainier Vista frames the mountain deliberately.
