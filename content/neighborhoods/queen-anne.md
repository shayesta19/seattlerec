Upper Queen Anne is residential and calm with a small commercial strip. The reason to come is Kerry Park — a small overlook that produces the shot of the Space Needle with Mount Rainier behind it that you have seen a hundred times. Lower Queen Anne, at the base of the hill, holds Seattle Center, Climate Pledge Arena, and the block where Seattle-style teriyaki started in 1976.
