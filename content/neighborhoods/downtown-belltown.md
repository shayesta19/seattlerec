Do the tourist run early in the day: Pike Place Market before 10am is a working market, and after 11am it is a crowd. The waterfront has been rebuilt into a proper park and promenade since the old viaduct came down. Pioneer Square at the south end has the best architecture in the city and the Underground Tour. Belltown is the bar strip immediately north, decent and slightly rough late.
