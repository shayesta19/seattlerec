South Seattle is where the city's immigrant communities have built, and the Rainier Valley is regularly cited as one of the most diverse zip codes in the United States. East African, Vietnamese, Filipino and Latin American food up and down MLK and Rainier Ave. Columbia City itself has a compact, landmarked main street with a farmers market and a historic movie theater.
