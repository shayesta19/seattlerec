## It is artsy first, tech second

There is a tech industry here and it is enormous, but it is **avoidable**. Whole neighborhoods have nothing to do with it. The city underneath is an arts town — music, printmaking, glass, theater, small galleries, weird public sculpture — and that is the version most locals live in.

## Music history it has not gotten over

**Grunge** came from here, and so did Jimi Hendrix. The venues that mattered mostly still stand. Live music remains one of the genuinely strong parts of the nightlife, in a city where the rest of the nightlife is not.

## Politics on the sleeve

Seattle is a place where **Black Lives Matter** organizing was early, visible, and sustained, and where street-level protest is a normal part of civic life. The politics are progressive, loud, and generally worn openly.

## The trolls

Danish artist **Thomas Dambo** built six giant trolls out of scrap wood across the region for *Northwest Trolls: Way of the Bird King*. They are free to visit and finding all of them is a genuinely good weekend:

- **Frankie Feetsplinter** — National Nordic Museum, Ballard
- **Bruun Idun** — Lincoln Park, West Seattle, near Colman Pool
- **Jakob Two Trees** — Issaquah, near the Rainier Trail
- **Pia the Peacekeeper** — Sakai Park, Bainbridge Island
- **Oscar the Bird King** — Point Robinson Park, Vashon Island
- plus one in Portland, Oregon

Two of them need a ferry. Check current locations before you go — some are temporary installations and they do move.

## City of dogs

There are somewhere around **150,000–180,000 dogs in Seattle and about 107,000 children.** Dogs outnumbering kids has been true since the late 1990s. The city is enormously dog-friendly: patios, breweries, off-leash parks, shops with a water bowl and a treat jar by the door.

## Bruce Lee

**Bruce Lee and his son Brandon are buried at Lake View Cemetery** on Capitol Hill, next to Volunteer Park. It is a quiet, well-visited grave and a short walk from one of the best views in the city.

## An entrepreneurial streak

Starbucks, Boeing, Amazon, Microsoft, Costco, Nordstrom, REI, Filson, Alaska Airlines — an unreasonable number of companies you have heard of started within an hour of here.

## Sports

**Seahawks** (NFL) and **Sounders FC** (MLS) and **Reign FC** (NWSL) at Lumen Field. **Kraken** (NHL) and **Storm** (WNBA) at Climate Pledge Arena. **Mariners** (MLB) at T-Mobile Park.

Two things to know: Sounders supporters are among the loudest in the league and the march to the match is worth seeing, and the Storm are one of the most successful franchises in the WNBA. There is no NBA team, and that remains a sore subject.
