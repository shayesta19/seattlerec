title: Fun facts
kicker: Things to say at a bar

summary: A short list of true, checkable, slightly improbable facts about this city.
---

- Seattle is **farther north than Toronto and Montreal**, which is why summer evenings stay light past 9pm and December afternoons end at 4:20pm.
- Two of the bridges across Lake Washington **float**. The lake is too deep and too soft on the bottom for conventional piers.
- The **Ballard Locks** move boats between salt water and fresh water, and have a fish ladder with a glass viewing window where you can watch salmon climb.
- Seattle ranks about **44th among major U.S. cities for annual rainfall** — behind New York, Boston, Houston, Miami, and Atlanta.
- **Dogs outnumber children** in the city, and have since the 1990s.
- Seattle, San Francisco, and Washington, D.C. are the only major U.S. cities where **fewer than 20% of households have children**.
- Downtown's **Pike Place Market** opened in 1907 and is one of the oldest continuously operating farmers markets in the country.
- The city sits near the **Cascadia Subduction Zone** and in view of **five active volcanoes**.
- **Seattle-style teriyaki** — charcoal chicken, sweet sauce, scoop of rice, cabbage salad — was popularized by a single shop that opened on Roy Street in 1976.
- The **Space Needle** was built for the 1962 World's Fair.
- Washington State Ferries is the **largest ferry system in the United States**, and for thousands of people it is simply the commute.
