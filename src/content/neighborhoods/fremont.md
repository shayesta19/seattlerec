Fremont leans hard into being odd and mostly pulls it off. There is a two-ton troll clutching a real Volkswagen under the Aurora Bridge, a Cold War rocket bolted to a storefront, and a Lenin statue rescued from Slovakia standing in a plaza. Underneath the bit, it is a pleasant canal-side neighborhood with good coffee, a summer street market, and an outdoor movie series.
