Ballard was a Nordic fishing community and has not entirely let go of it — the National Nordic Museum is here, and so is a troll. What it is now is one of the best eating and drinking neighborhoods in the city, with a dense run of breweries and a very good Sunday farmers market on Ballard Avenue. The Ballard Locks are a genuine attraction: boats changing water level, and salmon climbing a ladder behind glass. Golden Gardens beach is at the north end.
