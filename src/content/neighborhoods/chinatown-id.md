Come here hungry. The CID covers Chinatown, Japantown and Little Saigon, and the density of good, cheap, specific food is unmatched in Seattle. Uwajimaya is a full Asian grocery with a food hall attached. The neighborhood carries real history, including the WWII incarceration of its Japanese American residents, documented at the Wing Luke Museum.
