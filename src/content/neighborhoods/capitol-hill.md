If you only have one night out, have it here. Capitol Hill is where the bar scene actually is — dive bars, cocktail rooms, live music, late food — and it is the historic and current center of queer nightlife in Seattle. It is also genuinely walkable, which most of the city is not. Volunteer Park sits at the north end with a conservatory, a water tower you can climb for the view, and Lake View Cemetery next door.
