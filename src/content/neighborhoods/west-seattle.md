Take the water taxi rather than driving; it is faster, cheaper than parking, and the ride is the point. Alki Beach is a genuine sand beach with a paved path, and the sunset over the Olympics with the downtown skyline to your right is the best free thing in Seattle. Lincoln Park has trails, a saltwater pool, and one of the Dambo trolls.
